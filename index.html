Our story

We cook the way family cooks for a celebration: generously, carefully, and with something always left for seconds.

Dna Kitchen & Events started with a simple idea on Station Street: the food people line up for at a good party shouldn’t only come out once a year. So we opened a kitchen where it’s on the table every day.

Everything starts from scratch each morning, from slow braises and house marinades to sauces reduced by hand. The same team that runs our dining room plans and cooks for your events, so what you taste here is exactly what your guests get.
